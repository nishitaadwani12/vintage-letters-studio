Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

Your trust matters to us. This policy explains what information Asli Tohfa collects, why, and how we look after it.

What we collect

  • Order & contact details you give us: name, email, shipping address, phone (if provided), and the message, names, dates or instructions for your custom gift.
  • Payment information is collected and processed directly by our payment provider, Razorpay. We receive confirmation of payment but never see or store your full card or bank details.
  • Basic technical data such as your browser type, needed to serve the website.

How we use it

  • To make and deliver your order and to reply to your enquiries.
  • To send order-related updates (confirmation, dispatch, delivery).
  • To keep records required for accounting and tax.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send marketing emails without your consent.

Who we share it with

Only the trusted services that help us run the shop: Razorpay (payments), Formspree (delivering your form messages to our inbox), our hosting provider Vercel, and delivery couriers for shipping your order. Each processes your data only to provide their service to us.

Where your data lives in your browser

To make the site convenient, your cart and any form drafts are stored locally in your own browser (localStorage). This stays on your device and is not a server database.

Data retention

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfil the order and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Just email aslitohfa@gmail.com and we will help.

Contact

For any privacy question, reach us at aslitohfa@gmail.com or via our Contact page.
