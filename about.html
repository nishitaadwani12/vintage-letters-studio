Our Story

Bringing back the lost art of letter writing

In a world of instant messages, we believe some words deserve to be held. Inkwell & Ivy began at a kitchen table with a pot of tea-stained paper, a brass wax seal, and a stubborn belief that the most meaningful gifts are the ones made slowly.

Every piece we make is written, sealed, and pressed by hand in small batches - so your feelings arrive with the weight they deserve. A letter you can keep in a drawer and reread for years, long after the screen has gone dark.
