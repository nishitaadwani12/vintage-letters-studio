Shipping Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

Because each keepsake is made by hand, here's what to expect between placing your order and holding it.

Processing time

  • Ready items are packed and dispatched within 2-4 business days.
  • Personalised and custom pieces (letters, scrapbooks, hampers) take 5-8 business days to craft before dispatch, as they are made to order.
  • Larger bundles like the 12-Month Bundle or 365 Days jar may take a little longer - we'll always confirm the timeline by email.

Delivery estimates

  • We ship across India. Once dispatched, delivery usually takes 3-7 business days depending on your location.
  • Metro cities are typically quicker; remote pin codes may take a little longer.

Shipping charges

A flat shipping fee of ₹79 is added at checkout and shown before you pay.

Tracking

When your order ships, we email you a tracking link so you can follow it to your door. You can also reach us any time for an update.

Delays

Occasionally couriers face delays due to weather, festivals or local conditions that are outside our control. If your order seems held up, email us and we'll chase it for you.

Need it by a date?

Ordering for an anniversary, birthday or wedding? Tell us your deadline in the order notes or at aslitohfa@gmail.com and we'll do our best to make it arrive in time.
