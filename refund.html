Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

Every Asli Tohfa piece is handmade to order, often personalised with your words. This policy explains when an order can be cancelled or refunded. It is written to be fair to you while reflecting the nature of custom, made-by-hand work.

Cancellations

  • You may cancel for a full refund within 12 hours of placing your order, as long as we have not yet begun crafting it.
  • Once we have started work on a personalised item, it can no longer be cancelled, because it is made uniquely for you.
  • For non-personalised, ready items, you may cancel any time before dispatch for a full refund.

Damaged or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, faulty, or different from what you ordered, we will make it right. Email aslitohfa@gmail.com within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos. We will arrange a free repair, remake or full refund - whichever you prefer.

What isn't refundable

  • Personalised or custom items, unless they arrive damaged or made incorrectly on our part.
  • Errors in the text, names, dates or spelling you supplied at checkout (please double-check these before ordering).
  • Change-of-mind returns once a custom piece has been made or dispatched.

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method via Razorpay, typically within 5-7 business days. The time for the amount to appear depends on your bank or card issuer.

Need help?

We are a small studio and genuinely want you to love your gift. If something isn't right, talk to us at aslitohfa@gmail.com or through our Contact page and we will do our best to help.
