Our Story

Bringing back the lost art of letter writing

In a world of instant messages, we believe some words deserve to be held. Every Inkwell & Ivy piece is written, sealed, and pressed by hand in small batches — slow-crafted so that your feelings arrive with the weight they deserve. A letter you can keep in a drawer and reread for years.
